# LaunchLoop AI — Growth Campaign Plan
## "Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes"
**Campaign Objective:** Acquire 500 verified final-year engineering student workshop registrations in 7 days with a total budget of ₹2,000 (Target CAC: ₹4.00 per student).

---

## 1. Executive Summary & Strategy

The campaign targets final-year (2025/2026 graduating) engineering students across Tier-1, Tier-2, and Tier-3 Indian institutions who face urgent campus placement and capstone project deadlines.

Rather than relying on expensive, generic social media ads (which typically yield ₹40–₹80 CAC), LaunchLoop AI utilizes an **organic student ambassador network**, **placement group distribution**, and an **incentivized peer viral referral loop** powered by the **AI Project Magnet**. Paid budget (₹2,000) is reserved strictly as a targeted catalyst to accelerate early peer momentum and amplify late-stage urgency.

```
Total Registrations Target: 500 Students
Total Budget Allocated:     ₹2,000.00 INR
Target Blended CAC:         ₹4.00 INR per confirmed registrant
Timeline:                   7 Days (Monday Day 1 to Sunday Day 7)
```

### Detailed Daily Breakdown:
- **Day 1 (Monday) — Foundation & Seed (Target: 35 Registrations):**
  - Brief 20 campus ambassadors; distribute pre-approved WhatsApp copy variants.
  - Test registration flow and tracking links (`utm_source=whatsapp&utm_medium=ambassador`).
- **Day 2 (Tuesday) — Department Outreach Blast (Target: 65 Registrations, Cum: 100):**
  - Ambassadors post in 3rd/4th-year class and placement WhatsApp groups.

- **Day 3 (Wednesday) — LinkedIn & Placement Drive Push (Target: 75 Registrations, Cum: 175):**
  - Student ambassadors post placement-focused carousels on LinkedIn.
  - Reach out to college coding club leads (GDSC / ACM chapters).
- **Day 4 (Thursday) — Referral Loop Mid-Sprint Catalyst (Target: 85 Registrations, Cum: 260):**
  - First leaderboard update broadcasted to all registered students.

- **Day 5 (Friday) — Micro-Targeted Paid Story Push Begins (Target: 90 Registrations, Cum: 350):**
  - Launch ₹250/day Instagram Story ads geo-targeted to engineering colleges.
  - Emphasize weekend workshop timing: *"Sunday 7 PM — Code and deploy your AI project"*.
- **Day 6 (Saturday) — Urgency & Project Team Signups (Target: 95 Registrations, Cum: 445):**
  - Encourage capstone project teams of 3–4 students to register together.
  - Run second ₹250 Instagram ad burst.
- **Day 7 (Sunday) — Workshop Day Final Sprint (Target: 55 Registrations, Cum: 500):**
  - Final 6-hour reminder blast across WhatsApp groups: *"Seats closing at 6:00 PM"*.
  - Live session kicks off at 7:00 PM IST.

## 7. Ethical Referral Mechanics & Pre-Tested Copy

### Referral Incentive Structure:
- **Tier 1 (3 Referrals):** Priority Live Q&A Pass with the workshop instructor.
- **Tier 2 (5 Referrals):** Complete NxtWave AI Starter Kit Blueprint (code repo + deployment guide).
- **Tier 3 (10 Referrals):** 1-on-1 Placement Resume Review by NxtWave technical mentors.

### WhatsApp Sharing Copy (Student to Classmate):
> *"Hey! Our placement batch is attending this free workshop on Sunday: 'Build Your First AI Project in 60 Minutes'. You code a working prototype from scratch and get a verified certificate for your resume. Here is my invite link: http://localhost:5173/?ref=NXTAAR01"*

### Ethical Guardrails:
- No spam or automated bulk DM scrapers.
- No misleading salary or placement guarantees.
- Self-referrals strictly prevented at database level.
- Rate-limiting enforced on all sharing endpoints.

## 9. Key Assumptions & Risk Mitigations

1. **Assumption:** Campus ambassadors each deliver $\ge 8$ genuine registrations across their personal class networks.
   - *Mitigation:* Recruit 20 ambassadors (providing redundancy if 5 underperform).
2. **Assumption:** Referral engine achieves a viral k-factor of $\ge 0.30$ (30 referrals per 100 registrants).
   - *Mitigation:* The AI Project Magnet gives students a tangible project title to share with their project partners immediately upon registration.
3. **Assumption:** Free workshops face drop-off before attendance.
   - *Mitigation:* Focus acquisition copy on the *deliverable* (GitHub project + certificate) rather than generic attendance.
